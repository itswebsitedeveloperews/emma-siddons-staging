What does a buying agent do?+

I represent you, the buyer. I help define your brief, find and assess suitable homes, negotiate a purchase and support you through to completion. The selling agent represents the seller; I am there to look after your interests.

Can you help if I don’t know the area?+

Yes. We can start with what matters to you rather than a postcode. I help you focus your search across Hertfordshire, west Essex and South Cambridgeshire, considering how each location could work for your life.

Do you only search for off-market homes?+

No. I consider homes on the open market alongside private and pre-market opportunities. The important thing is how well a property fits your brief. Off-market availability varies, so I cannot promise a particular home will be available.

Will I work with you directly?+

Yes. My business is personal: you work directly with me throughout the search. I take on a small number of clients and avoid searches that would put my clients in direct competition with each other.

How do I get started?+

Call or email me and tell me a little about your plans. We can discuss where you are looking, your timing and the support you need. I will explain my service and agree the fee arrangement with you before any work begins.
