Areas I cover

Finding the right place is part of finding the right home.

Hertfordshire is at the heart of my work. I also help buyers find homes in west Essex and South Cambridgeshire.

We can start with a favourite village or a much broader brief. I’ll help you focus on the places that fit the way you want to live.

Hertfordshire

Local knowledge, built over years

Having lived in Hertfordshire for more than 40 years, I bring a personal understanding of its towns, villages and countryside to your search.

A home’s setting can be as important as the house itself. We can explore what matters to you, from village life and access to the countryside to schools, commuting and day-to-day convenience.
