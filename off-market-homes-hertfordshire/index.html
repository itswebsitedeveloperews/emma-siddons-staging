Discuss your off-market search

Looking beyond the listings

What does off-market mean?

An off-market home is offered privately, rather than advertised to everyone. A seller may value discretion, want to explore interest before launching, or prefer a quieter sale.

A pre-market opportunity is a little different: the property may be due to launch publicly, but an agent is already speaking to suitable buyers.

My local approach

Conversations open doors

A considered search

The right fit matters more than the label

Off-market does not automatically mean better value, and it does not guarantee an easier purchase. I look carefully at each home, its price and its suitability for you, whether it is being sold privately or openly.

Private opportunities change with the market and your brief. I cannot promise a particular property will be available, but I can help you search more widely and make an informed decision.

Your questions

A little more about buying privately

Can I see a list of off-market properties?

Private sellers often do not want their homes advertised. I discuss relevant opportunities with clients individually, based on their search brief and the seller’s wishes.

Do I need to be ready to buy?

It helps to be clear about your budget, timing and buying position. We can discuss these at the start so I can approach opportunities with a realistic understanding of your circumstances.

Will you also look at homes on the open market?

Yes. My search is led by what suits you. I consider publicly marketed homes as well as private and pre-market opportunities.
