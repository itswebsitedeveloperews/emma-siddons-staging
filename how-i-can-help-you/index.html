Working together

Your priorities.
My attention.

Every move is different. I tailor the search around you, offering a considered view of each opportunity and practical support at each stage.

As a buying agent, I work for you. My advice is independent of the seller and the selling agent.

Read about my service and fees →

  1. First, I listen

    We talk about the home you want, the way you live and the areas you are considering. I help you turn your priorities into a clear search brief, including what you can be flexible about.

  2. I look beyond the listings

    I research the market and speak with local agents and contacts. Alongside publicly advertised homes, I look for opportunities being discussed privately or before they come to market.

  3. I help you make sense of the options

    I can preview homes to save you time and accompany you on viewings. We look carefully at the property, its setting and how well it meets your brief.

  4. I negotiate on your behalf

    When the right opportunity comes along, I help you assess it and negotiate the price and terms. I represent your interests throughout the conversation.

  5. I stay involved through completion

    I liaise with agents, solicitors and other professionals to help keep the purchase progressing. You have someone who knows your search alongside you through exchange and completion.
