About me

Local knowledge. A personal perspective.

I’m Emma, an independent buying agent. I help you find and secure a home in Hertfordshire, west Essex and South Cambridgeshire.

I work for you, the buyer, with your priorities at the heart of the search.

Talk to me about your move

A place I know well

More than a postcode

Hertfordshire has been my home for more than 40 years. I know how much the feel of a place can change from one village to the next, and how important those differences are when choosing where to live.

I help you look beyond the particulars: the setting, the surrounding area and how a home could work for your everyday life.

My background

A considered approach

After studying law at UCL, I qualified as a chartered accountant with a Big Five firm and spent ten years in investment banking in the City.

That background informs the way I work: careful research, attention to detail and a clear head when it comes to negotiating a purchase.

Independent by choice

You work directly with me

I take on a small number of searches so I can give each client personal attention. From understanding your brief to keeping a purchase moving, I stay involved throughout.

My role is to be on your side: offering an independent view, helping you weigh up the options and knowing when a property is worth pursuing — or when it is time to walk away.
