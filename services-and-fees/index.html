My services & fees

Personal support, from the first conversation to the keys.

Finding a home takes time, local knowledge and a clear sense of what matters. I bring those together in a search shaped around you.

Discuss your property search

Search & acquisition

One service, throughout your move

I help you define your brief, research suitable homes and explore opportunities on and off the open market. I can preview properties for you, accompany you on viewings and offer an independent assessment of the options.

When you find the right home, I negotiate the price and terms on your behalf, then liaise with the people involved to help keep the purchase moving through exchange and completion.

Time for your search

A small number of clients

I work with a limited number of buyers so I can give each search the attention it needs. I do not take on clients whose requirements would put them in direct competition with one another.

Some searches come together quickly; others need more time. We will discuss your circumstances and agree the scope of my involvement before we begin.

My fees

Clear from the outset

My fee has two parts: an initial retainer to begin the search, followed by a success fee when you purchase. The exact fee and terms are agreed with you before I start work.

Get in touch to discuss the service that is right for your search.

Talk through my fees

Initial retainer

Typically £1,500, plus VAT.

Success fee

Typically 1–1.5% of the purchase price, plus VAT.

These are indicative fees. I will confirm the full fee arrangement and payment terms for your search in writing.
