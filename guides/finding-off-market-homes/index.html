My property-search guide

Finding a home beyond the property portals.

Waiting for a listing can leave you seeing only part of the picture. Here is how I approach a search that includes private and pre-market opportunities.

Start with what matters to you

Before looking for a property, get clear about the life you want it to support. Think about location, space, budget and timing, as well as the things you could compromise on. A focused brief makes it easier to recognise a suitable opportunity.

Understand why a home may be sold privately

Not every seller wants a public launch. Some value privacy; others want to explore demand before committing to a wider marketing campaign. An agent may also introduce a home to a few suitable buyers before it appears online.

These situations are different from one another. Understanding the seller’s intentions helps you decide how to approach the opportunity.

Keep conversations going

Off-market searches depend on people as much as property listings. I stay in touch with local agents and contacts, explain my clients’ requirements and explore relevant introductions. Where appropriate, I also approach homeowners directly.

Be clear about your buying position

A seller will want to understand your timing and whether you are in a position to proceed. Having a realistic budget and a clear picture of your own sale, if you have one, helps make those conversations productive.

Assess the home on its merits

A private sale is not automatically a bargain. Look at how the property meets your needs, its condition, the surrounding area and the asking price. The usual care and professional checks still matter.

I help my clients weigh up the opportunity, negotiate and keep the purchase progressing. My search includes both openly advertised and privately offered homes: the aim is to find the right fit for you.
